About

Most of my experience has been on a factory floor, where a solution is only as good as the trust users place in it on a day everything is going wrong.

Mechanical design brought me into automation, working on tooling, fixtures, dies and processes. I took on the controls and the data from there. That turns out to be the useful combination: the reason a deployed robot cell misses its target is rarely in the code. It could be a worn fixture, a chafed wire, or a broken sensor.

At Modineer I took ownership of production lines, shipping real fixes in code as well as tooling that led to measurable improvements. At Tesla I spent 9 months on production equipment during ramp-ups, which was a very fast and effective introduction to real design and manufacturing constraints.

I'm now at Carnegie Mellon in the MRSD program, developing my skills as a Robotics Engineer.

Other than all of that, I cook a lot, mostly Indian, though I fell in love with it backwards. I started cooking because I love eating, and I'm always down to try something new. The rest of the time I am on a badminton or pickleball court, or being sat on by my cat while playing video games.
