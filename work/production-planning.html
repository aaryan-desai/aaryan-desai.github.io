Paint Allocation & Floor Runlists

A planning tool for the paint line. Planning took at least 2–3 hours a shift, plus time hunting down containers; it now takes 30–45 minutes, with everything in one place.

Year2026
Planning time2–3+ h → 30–45 min
StackUnder construction
StatusIn use on the floor

What it does

  • Models routing and BOM as one directed graph.
  • Allocates painted stock by ship-date FIFO.
  • Publishes two floor runlists: one for powder coat (PC), one for e-coat (EC).

The front end was built with AI assistance.
