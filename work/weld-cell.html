Consolidation

Weld operations were split across stations, which meant parts moved between cells, each cell carried its own setup and changeover overhead, and the handoffs added queue time that never showed up as anyone's cycle time. Consolidating the operations into one cell removed the inter-cell transfers entirely.

The work was as much layout and tooling as it was robot programming: reach envelope, fixture placement and part presentation all had to be resolved before a consolidated sequence was even feasible. The result was a 12% increase in workflow efficiency and production capacity.

Tooling & die design

Alongside the cell work, I designed die components, precision fixtures and tooling specifically for repeatable robotic operation on complex geometries — parts where a human can compensate for a loose fixture and a robot simply cannot. Tight, well-datumed fixturing is what makes the faster motion profile usable in production rather than just achievable on a good day.
